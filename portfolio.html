📅 Business Problem Statement

Question: How can Superstore improve its sales performance by analyzing categories, regions, customer segments, and sales trends?

Objective: Analyze sales data and provide actionable business recommendations that can drive growth and improve decision-making.

Analytics Workflow
6-step end-to-end methodology
🗂️
Data Collection
Loaded Superstore CSV with 9,800 rows and 18 columns into Google Sheets for analysis.
🧹
Data Cleaning
Checked missing values, verified date formats, removed duplicates, and validated sales figures.
🛢️
SQL Analysis
Wrote SQL queries to aggregate sales by category, region, customer segment, and date.
📊
Data Visualization
Built bar charts, pie charts, and monthly line trend charts in Google Sheets dashboard.
💡
Business Insights
Extracted 4 key insights about top-performing categories, segments, and regions.
🎯
Recommendations
Delivered 3 actionable business recommendations aligned to the analytical findings.

Business Recommendations
Data-driven actions for growth
01
📦 Expand Technology Investment
Increase inventory and run targeted promotional campaigns for Technology products to capitalize on high demand. Explore bundling with Office Supplies to boost average order value.
02
🗺️ Boost South Region Marketing
Strengthen marketing and sales efforts in the South through localized campaigns, regional partnerships, and better product availability. Use West region strategies as a model.
03
👥 Consumer Loyalty Programs
Introduce loyalty rewards, personalized offers, and targeted advertising for Consumer customers to increase repeat purchases, retention, and lifetime customer value.
